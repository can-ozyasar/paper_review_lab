> Even the newest and largest AI language models still make factual mistakes. To solve this, a method called Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) was created. RAG gives the AI helpful articles to read before it answers, which helps it make fewer errors. However, RAG has some problems. It searches for information all the time, even when it does not need to. This can bring in useless or off-topic information, which makes the AI's answers worse. Also, the AI does not always use the information it finds because it was not trained to follow those facts.

To fix these issues, we created SELF-RAG. This new method helps the AI write better and more accurate answers. It does this by searching for information only when necessary and "thinking" about its own work. We train the AI to evaluate its own writing using special words called "reflection tokens." These tokens are split into two types: "retrieval tokens" (which tell the AI when to search for information) and "critique tokens" (which help the AI judge the quality of its own answers).

Here is how SELF-RAG works:
First, the AI decides if searching for extra information would be helpful. If so, it searches for relevant texts.
Second, it reads these texts, decides how useful they are, and writes a few different answers.
