> Even though large language models (LLMs) are very powerful, they often make up false information because they only rely what they memorized during trainingon . A method called Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) helps fix this by searching for outside information. However, searching for information every single time—even when it is not needed or not useful—can make the AI less flexible and lead to poor answers. 

To solve this, we introduce a new system called Self-Reflective Retrieval-Augmented Generation (SELF-RAG). This system helps the AI decide when it actually needs to search for information, and it checks both the search results and its own answers using special tags called reflection tags. These tags allow us to control the AI while it is generating an answer so it can fit different tasks. Experiments show that SELF-RAG performs much better than other top AI models on many different tasks, including answering questions, reasoning, and checking facts. It is also much better at being accurate and providing correct citations when writing long answers.
