> Even though newer AI language models can read very long pieces of text, we still do not know how well they actually understand and use all of that information. We tested these models on two tasks: answering questions from multiple documents and finding specific key-value data. We discovered that a model's performance drops a lot just by changing where the important information is placed. This shows that current AI does not use long texts very well. Specifically, models work best when the key facts are at the very beginning or the very end of the text. Their performance gets much worse when the important information is hidden in the middle, even for AIs specially built for long texts. Our research helps us better understand how AI reads long texts and creates new ways to test future AI models.
