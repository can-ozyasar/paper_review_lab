> First, we test how well AI models can answer questions using multiple documents. This is similar to how search tools like Bing Chat work, where the AI looks at several articles to find the answer. In our test, we control two things: how many documents we give the AI, and where the important information is located (at the beginning, middle, or end of the reading material). 

We discovered that the position of the information greatly changes how well the AI performs. This shows that current AI models are not very good at using information when it is hidden inside a long text. In fact, we found a "U-shaped" pattern in the results: the AI does a great job when the important facts are right at the beginning or very end of the text, but its performance drops significantly when the answer is buried in the middle.
