## Modellerin karşılaştırılması

![Görsel Analizi](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/can-ozyasar/paper_review_lab/main/notes/assets/crop_1789143208886.png)


> Figure 8 shows how AI models (specifically Flan-UL2 and Flan-T5-XXL) handle information based on where it is located in the text. 

When the text is short and within the length the models were trained on (under 2048 or 512 words/tokens), they do a good job no matter where the important information is placed (left graph). 

However, when the text is longer than what they were trained to handle (center and right graphs), their performance drops in the middle. They do much better when the important information is placed right at the beginning or the very end of the text, creating a U-shaped graph.
