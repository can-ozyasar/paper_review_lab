> New AI language models can read very long pieces of text, but we still do not know how well they actually understand and use all that information. We tested these models on two tasks: answering questions using multiple documents and finding specific information hidden in the text. We discovered that a model's performance drops a lot depending on where the important information is located, which shows that they are not very good at handling long texts yet. Specifically, these models do best when the important information is right at the beginning or the very end of the text. Their performance drops significantly when they have to find information hidden in the middle, even if they are designed for long texts. Our study helps us better understand how AI uses long texts and creates new tests to help improve future models.
